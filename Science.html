Department of Science

The Department of Science at Northbridge University promotes scientific learning, research, innovation and discovery. Our programmes are designed to develop strong theoretical knowledge and practical skills across various scientific disciplines.

Through laboratory work, research projects, field studies and modern learning methods, students gain the knowledge and experience required to explore scientific careers and contribute to technological and social development.
